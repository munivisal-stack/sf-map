How “empty” is defined here: an active SF Planning parcel for which the current DataSF building-footprint layer returns no building footprint on that parcel. This captures vacant land and many surface-parking/open-air sites, but it is not a legal vacant-land designation.

Zoning and height are joined from the official SF Planning GIS layers. Transit uses the City's Muni Stops dataset. The SF Planning parcel layer is current and updated by the City.
